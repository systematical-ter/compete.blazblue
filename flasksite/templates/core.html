About
The Wheel of Fate
This site is a web representation of the Wheel of Fate BBCF Event Calendar, maintained by Eterna. The events shown here are submitted by TOs and validated by Eterna before upload. Please join the Wheel of Fate server here if you'd like to submit events, or sign up to have the bot announce events to your discord server! Note that the server requires a manual verification process so it may take a bit to join.
